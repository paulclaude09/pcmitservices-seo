## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on all subpages** — Every crawled page returns identical `<title>`, `<meta description>`, and `<link rel="canonical" href="/">`. Google treats all URLs as duplicates of the homepage. This is the #1 ranking blocker.
2. **Location pages missing from sitemap** — `/it-services-dieppe` and `/it-services-riverview` return 200 but are not in sitemap.xml (18 URLs listed; neither location page included).
3. **Computer repair page not in sitemap** — `/services/computer-repair` returns 200 with strong content potential but is absent from sitemap.
4. **Utility pages indexed in sitemap** — `quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment` should be noindex and removed from sitemap.
5. **Sitemap intermittent 500** — Returns 200 via curl/Mozilla/Googlebot today; automated fetch tools still hit 500. Monitor in GSC.
