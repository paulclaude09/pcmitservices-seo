## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on all subpages (12th week unresolved)** — 15 tested pages return identical `<title>` (“IT Support Moncton | Managed IT | PCM IT Services”, 49 chars), identical meta description (128 chars), and canonical pointing to `/`. Google may still show differentiated titles for some URLs in SERPs (e.g. POS content), but **View Source** remains homepage SEO on every route — caps landing-page rankings and consolidates equity on `/`.
2. **Five utility pages still in sitemap** — `quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment` dilute crawl budget.
3. **No server-side H1 on any page** — Zero `<h1>` in raw HTML on all 15 tested URLs (Hostinger Horizons SPA).
4. **“IT support Moncton” visibility declined** — Homepage no longer appears in top-five organic snapshots; aligns with competitors’ unique per-URL SEO and stronger trust signals (reviews, FAQs, addresses).
