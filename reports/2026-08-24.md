## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on 17 pages** — Every subpage serves homepage title (`IT Support Moncton | Managed IT | PCM IT Services`), homepage meta, and canonical `/`. Google cannot index service or location pages for their target keywords.
2. **Location pages missing from sitemap** — `/it-services-dieppe` and `/it-services-riverview` return 200 but are not listed in `/sitemap.xml`.
3. **Utility/tool pages indexed in sitemap** — 5 internal tools (`quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment`) should be noindex and removed from sitemap.
4. **Sitemap intermittent 500** — Direct curl returns 200, but some crawlers/fetchers hit HTTP 500. Monitor in GSC; re-submit sitemap after SEO fixes.
