## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on all subpages (11th week unresolved)** — 13 tested pages return identical `<title>` (“IT Support Moncton | Managed IT | PCM IT Services”, 49 chars), identical meta description (128 chars), and canonical pointing to `/`. Google may still rewrite titles for some URLs (e.g. backup, POS) from rendered content, but **View Source** remains homepage SEO on every route — caps landing-page rankings and keeps Dieppe/Riverview/repair/POS URLs from competing.
2. **Five utility pages still in sitemap** — `quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment` dilute crawl budget.
3. **No server-side H1 on any page** — Zero `<h1>` in raw HTML on all 13 tested URLs (Hostinger Horizons SPA).
4. **POS + location keywords still invisible** — No PCM URL in top results for “POS system setup Moncton,” “IT services Dieppe,” or “IT services Riverview”; aligns with duplicate canonicals pointing all equity to `/`.
