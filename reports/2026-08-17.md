# SEO Audit — 2026-08-17

## Executive summary

Major progress since July: `/sitemap.xml` now returns **200** (was 500), and the homepage has proper server-side title, meta description, canonical, and LocalBusiness schema with `support@pcmitservices.com`. However, a new **Critical** issue emerged — Hostinger serves the **same homepage SEO tags on every URL**, with all pages canonicalizing to `/`. Core keywords (`IT support Moncton`, `managed IT services Moncton`, `computer repair Moncton`) remain Page 2+ while BeckTek, NetAdmins, and digiBandit dominate. Priority this week: per-page SEO in Hostinger, noindex utility pages, and publish Dieppe/Riverview location pages.

## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on all 18 pages** — Every URL returns identical `<title>`, `<meta description>`, and `<link rel="canonical" href="/">` (homepage URL on every page). Subpages like `/it-support-moncton` and `/services/wifi-networking-setup` tell Google they are duplicates of the homepage. **Impact:** Service and location pages cannot rank independently.
2. **Utility pages indexed in sitemap** — `quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment` are in `/sitemap.xml` with no `noindex`. **Impact:** Crawl budget waste, thin/duplicate content signals.
3. **Missing location pages in sitemap** — `/it-services-dieppe` loads (200) but is **not** in sitemap; no `/it-services-riverview` page found. BeckTek has dedicated Dieppe and Riverview pages ranking above PCM.

## Action list

**1. Fix duplicate canonical/title/meta on all subpages**
- **Issue:** Every page tells Google it IS the homepage. Subpages cannot rank for target keywords.
- **Fix (Hostinger):** For each page → **Page Settings → SEO Settings** → set unique Title, Meta Description, and Canonical URL matching the page's actual path. Repeat for all 12 public-facing pages (exclude utility pages).
