## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on all subpages (9th week unresolved)** — 12 tested pages return identical `<title>` (“IT Support Moncton | Managed IT | PCM IT Services”, 49 chars), identical meta description (128 chars), and canonical pointing to `/`. Google may render some unique titles in SERPs (e.g. data backup, POS) from client-side content, but **View Source still shows homepage SEO** — indexing and ranking remain at risk.
2. **Five utility pages still in sitemap** — `quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment` dilute crawl budget and may index thin/internal tools.
3. **No server-side H1 on any page** — Zero `<h1>` tags in raw HTML on all 13 tested pages (Horizons SPA client-render only).
4. **POS page duplicate canonical** — Only keyword with consistent top-10 visibility; server-side canonical still `/` while competitors (RKP, digiBandit) have unique page-level SEO.
