## Critical issues

1. **Duplicate server-side SEO on all subpages (8th week unresolved)** — 12 tested pages return identical `<title>` ("IT Support Moncton | Managed IT | PCM IT Services", 49 chars), identical meta description (128 chars), and canonical pointing to `/`. Google cannot rank service or location pages until each has unique server-side SEO in Hostinger Page Settings.
2. **Location pages + computer repair missing from sitemap** — `/it-services-dieppe`, `/it-services-riverview`, and `/services/computer-repair` return 200 but are not in sitemap.xml (18 URLs listed; none of these three).
3. **Five utility pages still in sitemap** — `quote-builder`, `client-onboarding`, `ai-agent-requirements`, `ai-readiness-assessment`, `it-service-assessment` dilute crawl budget and may index thin/internal tools.
4. **No server-side H1 on any page** — Zero `<h1>` tags in raw HTML on all 13 tested pages (Horizons SPA client-render only).
5. **POS visibility at risk** — Only keyword with top-10 visibility; duplicate canonical on POS page likely caps further gains while merchant/POS competitors expand content.
